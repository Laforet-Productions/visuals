Privacy Policy

VISUALS app for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 22, 2026

VISUALS is a photography app by Vincent Laforet, published by Laforet Productions Inc. ("we"). This policy explains what the app and its download service handle, and what they do not.

The short version

  • No account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising software in the app.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device only: which collections you have downloaded, the photographs themselves, and the date and version of the terms you accepted. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. When you use a purchase to export a wallpaper, the app sends Apple's signed record of that purchase (which includes Apple's transaction and product identifiers, not your name or Apple Account details) to our download service. The service checks that record with Apple and then provides the one photograph you are entitled to. To stop a download link from being reused, the service stores a scrambled (one-way hashed) code with an expiry time of a few minutes; these records are deleted automatically.

Download service and server logs

Free collection downloads and wallpaper files are delivered from our service hosted on Google Cloud. Like most web services, Google Cloud keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, time and the file requested) for operating and securing the service. We do not use them to identify or profile you, and they are deleted on Google Cloud's standard schedule (about 30 days).

Photos

If you save a wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add one image to your photo library. It cannot read, browse or upload your existing photos.

Children

VISUALS is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel a subscription, and request refunds, through your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. You can remove all app data by deleting the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date. Questions: web@vincentlaforet.com.
