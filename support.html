VISUALS Support

Help for the VISUALS app on iPhone and iPad

Questions, problems or feedback: web@vincentlaforet.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Browsing

Tap the screen to show or hide the controls. Swipe left and right to move between photographs, and up and down to change collections. Tap Info for the caption and camera details, or Play for a slideshow.

Downloading collections

Some collections are included with the app. The others are free to download for viewing: open the collection and tap Download Free. Downloads need an internet connection; once finished, the collection stays on your device.

Wallpapers and purchases

Death Valley Dunes wallpapers are included free. For the other collections you can unlock wallpaper export for one collection, or for every collection with All Collections annual access. After unlocking, tap Wallpaper to frame the photograph and save it to Photos, then set it as your wallpaper from the Photos app or Settings. Apple shows the price before you confirm.

Restoring purchases

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Help & Manual and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

Subscriptions and refunds

All Collections renews yearly until canceled. You can cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

VISUALS has no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
