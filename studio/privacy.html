VISUALS STUDIO

Privacy Policy

VISUALS Studio app for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 24, 2026

VISUALS Studio is an app by Vincent Laforet ("we") for building your own photo galleries, presenting them and sharing them. This policy explains what the app handles, and what it does not.

Data Not Collected. We do not collect any data from the app. Your photographs and galleries stay on your device, and nothing is sent to us.

The short version

  • No account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising software in the app.
  • VISUALS Studio has no server of its own. The app does not upload your photographs anywhere.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.

Your photographs and galleries

You add photographs with Apple's photo picker, so the app receives only the photographs you choose; it cannot browse the rest of your library. The app copies the photographs you choose into its own private storage on your device, together with your gallery names and order and the studio name you enter. It also keeps smaller copies of your photographs in its cache so galleries scroll smoothly. All of this stays on your device. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's terms, and deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

When you share photographs or a PDF of a gallery, the app prepares the files on your device and hands them to the iOS share sheet. They go only where you choose to send them (for example AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files), under the terms of that service. The temporary copies are deleted when the share sheet closes.

Purchases

Studio Unlimited purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The app checks what you have bought with Apple, on your device. We do not receive your name, email address or Apple Account details. Apple gives us sales reports that do not identify you.

Email to support

If you write to us, we use your email address and what you send only to reply to you.

Children

VISUALS Studio is not directed to children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel a subscription, and request refunds, through your Apple Account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. You can remove all app data by deleting the app; keep your original photographs in your photo library.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date. Questions: production@vincentlaforet.com.
