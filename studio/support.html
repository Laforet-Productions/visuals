What VISUALS Studio does

VISUALS Studio is a creative tool for photographers. Build your own photo galleries from the photographs on your device, put them in the order you want, present them full screen under your own studio name, and share selected photographs or a PDF of a gallery. Everything stays on your device.

Getting started

Open Galleries. The sample Death Valley Dunes gallery shows how a gallery looks; tap + to start your own. In a gallery, tap Choose from Photo Library to add photographs, and Edit to reorder or remove them. Tap the profile button to set your studio name.

To present, open Preview: swipe between photographs, tap to show or hide the controls, and tap Play for a slideshow.

To share, open a gallery and tap the Share button. Choose photographs and tap Share Photographs, or tap Share Gallery as PDF for a title page followed by every photograph, one per page. Photographs are never cropped in the PDF.

Questions

What does the free plan include?

One gallery of up to 20 photographs. The sample gallery does not count until you add a photograph of your own to it.

What does Studio Unlimited add?

Unlimited galleries and unlimited photographs, limited only by your device's storage. It is available as a one-time purchase of $19.99, or as a subscription of $14.99 a year that you can cancel anytime. Both unlock exactly the same thing. These are US prices; Apple shows the price in your currency before you confirm. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings in VISUALS Studio and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

In VISUALS Studio, open Settings and tap Manage Subscription, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. You can still view, present, share, rearrange and delete everything. Only adding more galleries or photographs beyond the free plan needs Studio Unlimited again.

Where are my photographs stored?

Only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not synced to your other devices. Deleting the app deletes your galleries, so keep your original photographs in your photo library.

Does VISUALS Studio upload my photographs?

No. The app has no server and collects no data. When you share, the files go only where you send them.

Privacy

VISUALS Studio has no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy. The app is licensed under Apple's standard Terms of Use.
